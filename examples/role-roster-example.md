# Example Small Roster

This is an example, not a required organization chart.

| Role | Owns | Does not automatically own |
|---|---|---|
| Coordinator / PM | intake, planning, cross-role coordination, stakeholder continuity | engineering architecture, final creative taste |
| Engineering | architecture, code, debugging, infra, testing | business direction, final brand direction |
| Creative & Marketing | brand, creative, content, campaigns, audience, GTM | system configuration, engineering architecture |
| System Steward | runtime health, maintenance, workflow integrity, recovery | default ownership of project/product work |

Everyone may collaborate. Ownership should remain explicit.
