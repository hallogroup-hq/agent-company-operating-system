# 5. Build an Authority Matrix

## Goal

Membuat autonomy aman karena agent tahu apa yang boleh dikerjakan tanpa meminta izin.

## Three zones

### Autonomous

Safe, reversible, in-role actions.

### Coordinate / review

Boleh dikerjakan tetapi perlu specialist/reviewer/owner lain.

### Founder Gate

Harus meminta founder karena menyentuh authority atau risk yang tidak boleh diasumsikan.

## Typical Founder Gates

- uang / paid subscription / spend;
- legal atau financial commitment;
- important public/external commitment;
- credential/security change;
- destructive or irreversible operation;
- deletion of valuable/canonical data;
- risky production change;
- fundamental objective/scope change;
- final major business direction;
- final taste-dependent brand direction;
- protected identity/canon;
- authority-model changes.

## Step-by-step

Untuk setiap role, tulis:

1. 10-20 autonomous actions.
2. 5-10 coordination actions.
3. explicit prohibited/founder-gated actions.
4. reversible vs irreversible boundary.
5. what "safe diagnostic" means.

## Useful rule

Authority harus **mengurangi founder involvement**, bukan menciptakan reckless autonomy.
